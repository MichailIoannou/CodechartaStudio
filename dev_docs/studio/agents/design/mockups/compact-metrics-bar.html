Compact metrics bar

02-themes T4 compact bar, owner answer to question 12. When less than 30rem of map is free between the open side panels, the metrics bar turns into icon buttons in two rows, about as tall as the full bar. It returns at 32rem. Every button has a tooltip and opens the same popover as its card.

1 · Full bar at 1400 px, compact bar at 900 px (both panels open, 320 px each)

Left: 1400 px window, 748 px of map free, full bar. Right: 900 px window, 236 px free, compact bar (231 px). Both are drawn at half size.

2 · Full bar up close: cell icons instead of the cog

Each card's settings button shows the cell's own icon and opens the same settings popover; title "Area settings", "Height settings", …. Scenarios shows its bookmark, which opens the same menu as in the compact bar; Layout keeps its glyph and caret.

3 · Compact bar up close (dark and light)

Groups: Layout | Area, Height, link / Edges, Color | Labels / Scenarios | view tools. The link button stands beside Height and Color, the two cells it joins. Shown at 1.6×.

4 · Hover, open chooser, Scenarios menu

A tooltip shows on hover and on keyboard focus and doubles as the aria-label. Clicking a metric icon opens that card's metric chooser, which in the compact bar ends with "<cell> settings". Scenarios opens a two-item menu.
